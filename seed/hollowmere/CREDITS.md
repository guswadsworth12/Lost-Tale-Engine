# Hollowmere Station art credits

All art in this directory was created for Hollowmere Station on 2026-10-02 using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. It is original project art, not taken from an existing game, franchise, website, or private world. The final PNGs were cropped from the generated contact sheets, resized, and palette-optimized for the seed. The project releases these assets under the repository's [MIT license](../../LICENSE).

| Files | Source and brief |
| --- | --- |
| `backgrounds/*.png` | Generated four-panel station environment sheet: platform, waiting room, signal box, marsh edge; painterly teal and amber rural railway scenes, no people. |
| `backgrounds-night/*.png` | Generated edit of that sheet with matching night lighting and unchanged locations. |
| `sprites/mara/*.png` | Generated eight-expression sheet of original stationmaster Mara Vale; portrait is a crop of her neutral sprite. |
| `sprites/tavi/{neutral,happy,sad,angry,surprised,scared,thinking,determined,avatar}.png` | Generated eight-expression sheet of original courier Tavi Rook; portrait is a crop of their neutral sprite. |
| `sprites/tavi/fox--*.png` | Generated four-pose sheet of Tavi's original russet fox form. |
| `sprites/passenger/*.png` | Generated eight-expression sheet of the hooded passenger; portrait is a crop of the neutral sprite. |
| `sprites/rowan/*.png` | Generated eight-expression sheet of original player traveler Rowan Hale; portrait is a crop of the neutral sprite. |

No music or third-party art is bundled with this world.
