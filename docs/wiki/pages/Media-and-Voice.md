# Media and voice

**Media** is a library for story moments, character CGs, and world soundtracks. It is not a dump of every image file: Visual Novel sprites and outfits stay with their individual characters in **Cast → Presentation**, and location backgrounds stay with their worlds.

## Make a story image

Choose **Picture this** on a message or in scene tools. Pick a moment, a location background, or a portrait. The draft prompt uses the line, scene, time of day, the current cast's appearance and forms, and the world's art style. Review the prompt and who is included before sending it to the image service. In **Settings → Images**, you can choose whether everyone present starts included and whether the story model improves the draft automatically.

A moment appears under its message, on the Visual Novel stage, and under **Media → Story moments**, grouped by story, chapter, and scene. **Go to moment** returns to its source. A background or portrait can also be offered to the world or character; replacing an existing asset asks first. Deleting a scene permanently deletes its moments.

## Connect providers

Image and speech features are optional. Configure them under **Settings → Images** and **Settings → Voice**; test the service before relying on it in a session. Hosted providers receive the content you send them and may charge for generation. Generated art is previewed before save. Back up saved media with your data folder or app backup.

See [Models and Services](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Models-and-Services) for connection setup and [Backup, Restore and Privacy](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Backup-Restore-and-Privacy) for storage.
