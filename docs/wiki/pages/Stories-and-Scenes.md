# Stories and scenes

A **story** is your ongoing roleplay. It contains chapters, and each chapter contains numbered scenes. Breaking play into scenes gives the model a manageable recent transcript while recaps carry the important context forward.

## Start or resume

1. Open **Stories → Start a story**.
2. Choose a world or **Freeform · no world**, then the main character and any additional cast.
3. Choose whom you play and an opening style and line. Select **Begin story**.

Open a story card to resume its current scene. Cards show the world, cast, recent activity, and current goal or last line. Pin frequently used stories. The card's **Story actions** menu contains management actions, including forking and deleting; inspect the confirmation before deleting.

## Move through a long story

Open **Story panel → Scenes** to revisit earlier scenes. **End scene** records a recap and opens the next scene. From the same dialog, **End chapter** records a chapter recap and open threads, then opens a new chapter with an optional name and goal. Read and correct recap text before carrying it forward when the option is offered: a bad summary can distort later play.

Fork a story to explore a different branch. Rewind or fork from a message when you want a different consequence; later branch state does not travel backward. The current story remains available from the main menu while you play.
