# Lost Tales Engine

Build a world, bring a cast to life, and play a story whose choices and rolls matter. This guide covers the current app for players, people hosting it, and contributors.

## Choose a starting point

- **New player:** [Quick Start](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Quick-Start) takes you from first sign-in to the bundled Hollowmere story.
- **Making a setting:** [Worlds and Modules](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Worlds-and-Modules), [Cast and Character Sheets](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Cast-and-Character-Sheets), and [Writer's Room](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Writers-Room).
- **Running a server:** [Installation and Docker](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Installation-and-Docker), [Accounts and Sharing](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Accounts-and-Sharing), and [Backup, Restore and Privacy](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Backup-Restore-and-Privacy).
- **Working on the code:** [Architecture and Data Flow](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Architecture-and-Data-Flow) and [Contributing and Testing](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Contributing-and-Testing).

The sidebar lists every guide. **Help & tutorial** inside the app is a searchable quick reference and guided tour; this wiki adds full workflows and troubleshooting. [Known Limits and Roadmap](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Known-Limits-and-Roadmap) separates shipped behavior from future work.
