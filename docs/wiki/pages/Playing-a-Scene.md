# Playing a scene

The play surface has a transcript or Visual Novel stage, a composer, and the **Story panel**. Write your character's action or dialogue, or press **Continue** to let the cast and Game Master advance. The Game Master chooses who acts next in group scenes; it must leave your character's decisions to you.

## During a turn

1. Check the current speaker and scene goal.
2. Describe what you try. Use **Make a move / roll** when the world's mechanical rules call for a check.
3. Read the result before choosing the next action. A success may establish what you learned while leaving follow-up questions and choices open.
4. If a reply misstates a fact, correct it in the next message or use the story's edit, rewind, or fork controls where appropriate.

The **Story panel** gathers **Scene**, **Scenes**, **Goals**, **People**, **Sheet**, **State**, **Canon**, **Notes**, and **Scene Rules**. Sheet and State appear when the story has rules to show. Use Goals and Notes for intent that should survive a scene transition; use Canon for confirmed shared facts.

## Classic and Visual Novel

Classic is a readable transcript. Visual Novel puts cast art over a location background and dialogue box. Switch presentation from the play controls. On desktop, the app menu remains a compact rail; in Visual Novel open **Scene controls → Main menu** to expand it. On a phone, the menu opens as a drawer while the bottom navigation is hidden during play.

For a failed or missing roll, see [Game Master and Rolls](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Game-Master-and-Rolls). For stage layout and forms, see [Visual Novel and VRM](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Visual-Novel-and-VRM).
