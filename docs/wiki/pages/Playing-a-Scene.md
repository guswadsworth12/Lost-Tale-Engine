# Playing a scene

The play surface has a transcript or Visual Novel stage, a message box, and the **Story panel**. Write your character's action or dialogue and press Enter. In group scenes the Game Master chooses who acts next; it must leave your character's decisions to you.

![Classic transcript view of the Hollowmere story: Rowan asks questions, Mara and Tavi reply, each line noting who will remember it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/master/screenshots/wiki-hollowmere-transcript.jpg)

## During a turn

1. Check the current speaker and scene goal.
2. Describe what you try. Use **Make a move / roll** when the world uses **Roll for outcomes** and the moment calls for a check.
3. Read the result before choosing the next action. A success may establish what you learned while leaving follow-up questions and choices open.
4. To hear from someone in particular, pick them in **Reply as** (next to the message box) before you send; in Visual Novel mode the **Reply as** buttons in the scene controls do the same.
5. With the box empty, **Continue** extends the last reply rather than starting a new one; **Undo last continue** takes it back.
6. If a reply misstates a fact, correct it in your next message or use the message's **Regenerate**, **Steer this reply**, **Rewind to here**, or **Fork chat from here**.

Each line shows who will remember it. Characters only remember what happened while they were present, and a character who enters the story as someone new doesn't know anyone's name until they've heard it said.

## The Story panel

The **Story panel** gathers **Scene**, **Scenes**, **Goals**, **People**, **Sheet**, **State**, **Canon**, **Notes**, and **Scene Rules**. **Sheet** and **State** appear only when the world's rules have something to show. Use Goals and Notes for intent that should survive a scene change; use Canon for confirmed shared facts.

![The Story panel's Scene tab showing location, atmosphere, the day and time, and who is here now](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/master/screenshots/wiki-story-panel.jpg)

## Classic and Visual Novel

Classic is a readable transcript. Visual Novel puts cast art over a location background with a dialogue box. Switch between them with **Switch to transcript view** / **Switch to Visual Novel view**. On desktop the app menu stays a compact rail; in Visual Novel, open **Scene controls → Main menu** to expand it. On a phone, the menu opens as a drawer and the bottom navigation is hidden during play.

For a failed or missing roll, see [Game Master and Rolls](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Game-Master-and-Rolls). For stage layout and forms, see [Visual Novel and VRM](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Visual-Novel-and-VRM).
