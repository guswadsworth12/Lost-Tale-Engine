# Hollowmere Station

Hollowmere Station is the bundled Visual Novel starter world. Rowan Hale arrives on the last train to help stationmaster Mara Vale inventory a small rural station. Courier Tavi Rook is on the platform, and a hooded passenger carries a brass ticket with a blank name line. The marsh signal lamp is dark.

Every image here is a screenshot of the real app, taken on a fresh install with only the bundled content. The replies were written by a local model (Gemma 4 E4B through Ollama), not by hand; yours will differ.

### Visual Novel opening

![Hollowmere in Visual Novel mode: Mara, Tavi, and the hooded passenger on the night platform, Tavi introducing themself to Rowan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/master/screenshots/wiki-hollowmere-visual-novel.jpg)

The story opens on the night platform with Mara's first line. Rowan is the player character: a "you only" card that the cast never speaks or acts for.

### Story arc

1. **The last train:** Bring the passenger inside, inspect the blank ticket, and learn that the signal lamp is dark.
2. **The signal box:** Find a dry wick and oil, then relight the signal lamp.
3. **The marsh edge:** Follow the answering light and return safely.

### World and cast

![Hollowmere's Locations tab: the platform, waiting room, signal box, and marsh edge backgrounds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/master/screenshots/wiki-world-locations.jpg)

The world includes the platform, waiting room, signal box, and marsh edge, each with day and night art. The cast is Mara, Tavi (with a human and a fox form), the hooded passenger, and player character Rowan. Hollowmere uses **Guided outcomes** for its story rules, with relationships, Visual Novel presentation, and world simulation turned on and dating tools off. It's shared with every account on the server.

![Tavi Rook's Presentation tab, with the Base and Fox appearances and the expression sprites](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/master/screenshots/wiki-cast-presentation.jpg)

## Play it

Follow [Quick Start](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Quick-Start). A configured text model is needed for new AI replies; the bundled world, cast, opening line, and art work without one.

The world's **Story Rules → Game Master continuity notes** hold the mystery's answer. Don't open them if you want to play it fresh.

The story and images use original project art. See the [Hollowmere art credits](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/blob/master/seed/hollowmere/CREDITS.md).
