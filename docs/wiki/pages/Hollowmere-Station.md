# Hollowmere Station

Hollowmere Station is the bundled Visual Novel starter world. Rowan Hale arrives on the last train to help stationmaster Mara Vale inventory a small rural station. Courier Tavi Rook is on the platform, and a hooded passenger carries a brass ticket with a blank name line. The marsh signal lamp is dark.

## Product gallery

These are **illustrative product mockups**, composed from the Hollowmere art bundled in the repository. They show the intended stage, story, and world views; they are not live screenshots or a model-generated playthrough. No personal story data appears in them.

### Story arc

![Mock story and chapter overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/master/screenshots/hollowmere-story-mock.jpg)

1. **The last train:** Bring the passenger inside, inspect the blank ticket, and notice the dark signal.
2. **The signal box:** Find a dry wick and oil, then relight the signal lamp.
3. **The marsh edge:** Follow the answering light and return safely.

### World and cast

![Mock Hollowmere world overview with four locations and four characters](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/master/screenshots/hollowmere-world-mock.jpg)

The seed includes the platform, waiting room, signal box, and marsh edge, each with day and night art. The cast includes Mara, Tavi (human and fox forms), the hooded passenger, and player character Rowan. Hollowmere uses guided story rules, relationships, and Visual Novel presentation.

## Play it

Follow [Quick Start](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Quick-Start). A configured text model is needed for new AI replies; the bundled world, cast, opening lines, and art are available locally.

The story and images use original project art. See the [Hollowmere art credits](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/blob/master/seed/hollowmere/CREDITS.md).
