# Relationships and dating

**Relationships** adds connections and relationship details to a world. **Dating tools** add date, gift, intimacy, and CG systems. Turn them on in **Worlds → Overview → World modules** for stories that need them. A Dating Sim template puts romance up front; other templates can let it develop naturally, and dating can be off entirely.

## Configure and play

1. Define the cast's starting connections in the world or character relationship editors.
2. If dates or gifts matter, enable Dating tools and review its options before starting a story.
3. In play, open **Story panel → People** to inspect connections. Dating details and **Bag and gifts** appear when the world enables those controls.
4. Let relationship changes follow actions and confirmed story events rather than assuming every friendly exchange is romance.

The module choice also controls the dating chrome and prompt emphasis. A world with dating off should not display dating controls in play. Switching a module off keeps its data for later. CG unlocks are browsed in **Media**; character sprites remain in **Cast**.

See [Worlds and Modules](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Worlds-and-Modules) for the module selector.

In a group scene, each character's private lore unlocks on their own affection with you, not the lead's. See [Lore and Knowledge](https://github.com/guswadsworth12/Lost-Tale-Engine/wiki/Lore-and-Knowledge).
